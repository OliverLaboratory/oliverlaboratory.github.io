Interactive tools and data from our papers.

BioBlobs enzyme explorer

Wang, Shi & Oliver · NeurIPS 2026

BioBlobs splits a protein into a few spatially local groups of residues, called blobs, and learns which ones matter for its function. Browse the blobs it found on 883 enzymes from the Mechanism and Catalytic Site Atlas (M-CSA) in 3D, next to their annotated catalytic residues.

UNICORNE

Vanderbilt-Fried, Butskhrikidze, Brown, Mchaourab & Oliver · NeurIPS 2026

A database of conformational ensembles built from the PDB and grouped by UniProt entry: more than 940,000 structures across 54,000 proteins. Watch each protein move with normal-mode animations and superposed conformations, browse by ECOD, CATH or InterPro fold, and compare sequence-to-ensemble predictors on the UNICORNE-BENCH leaderboard.

Ensembits

Shi & Oliver · NeurIPS 2026

A learned tokenizer for protein conformational ensembles. Ensembits turns an ensemble of backbone conformations into one discrete token per residue that captures how much and how that site moves, and decodes the token back to backbone frames. The release includes the trained model, a script to tokenize your own ensembles, and the downstream benchmarks from the paper.

GitHub → Paper

RNAglib

Mallet, Oliver et al. · Bioinformatics 2022

A Python package for RNA 3D structures. It gives quick access to every available RNA 3D structure with base pair, binding and modification annotations, represents RNAs as 2.5D graphs, and provides ready-made machine learning benchmarks on seven structure-function tasks.

Open the docs → Paper GitHub

ProteinShake

Kucera, Oliver, Chen & Borgwardt · NeurIPS 2023

A Python package that loads large, preprocessed protein structure datasets in one line, with protein- and residue-level labels, splits by sequence and structure similarity, and metrics. It draws on the RCSB PDB and AlphaFold DB (more than 500,000 structures), converts them to graphs, voxels or point clouds for any major framework, and has a leaderboard of benchmark tasks.
